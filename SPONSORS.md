# Sponsor WallpaperMachine

WallpaperMachine is free and open source. The signed download costs nothing,
and the complete source is here to build.

It is a project of [MMFoundation](https://github.com/MMFoundation), the
open-source organization that also makes OhMyShot. Sponsoring happens on
MMFoundation's Open Collective, where every contribution and expense is public.

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://opencollective.com/mmfoundation"><b>Sponsor on Open Collective</b></a>
</p>

## Sponsor MMFoundation.

On [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/mmfoundation) you can give once or
every month. The money goes to MMFoundation and backs its work on WallpaperMachine
and OhMyShot. Every contribution, and every expense with its receipt, is public
there. The tiers are recognition only: sponsoring unlocks nothing, and the app stays
the same for everyone. Sponsors appear in the
[README](README.md#sponsors-on-open-collective), a company with its logo and a link and
a person with their avatar, as Open Collective draws them.

## Priority Support.

Priority Support is a paid service, sold on the website's
[pricing page](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/pricing/)
([中文](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/zh/pricing/)). It isn't a way to sponsor
the project, and its money doesn't pass through the Collective.

- **Your questions first.** Ask about a wallpaper that doesn't render right, or a
  feature you would like, on the
  [support forum](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/support/). Your questions are
  answered ahead of general requests.
- **One payment.** No subscription and nothing to renew.
- **Nothing to unlock.** It buys no software: no feature, no license key and no
  special build. The app works the same with or without it, buyers get the same
  GPL-3.0-only license as everyone else, and a refund takes none of it back. The
  reasons are in
  [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md#priority-support-a-paid-service-not-the-software).
- **14-day money-back guarantee.** The
  [refund policy](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/refunds/) explains how to ask
  for one.
- **Terms.** Buying means agreeing to the
  [terms](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/terms/) and the
  [privacy notice](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/privacy/). They cover the
  purchase only; the GPL still governs the app and its source.

The pricing page shows the price in your currency, tax included. Paddle.com handles
the payment as reseller and Merchant of Record, and a free account on the website
links the purchase to you.

### The thank-you wall.

People who bought Priority Support under its earlier name, Supporter, were promised
a sponsor place: a name and picture on the website's
[thank-you wall](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/#sponsors) and in the
[README's list](README.md#thank-you-to-every-priority-support-member). That listing
stays, and they can still change it from their account.

New buyers can choose to appear on the thank-you wall. It's off by default and isn't
part of what they buy.

## Other ways to help.

- **Star the repository**, so more Mac users find it.
- **Report a wallpaper that looks wrong.** **Not working? Report on GitHub** in
  its details opens an [issue](https://github.com/WallpaperMachine/WallpaperMachine/issues)
  with the wallpaper's information filled in.
- **Send a fix.** [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how to work on the
  code.
- **Tell a friend** who wants a moving desktop on their Mac.
