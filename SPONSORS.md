# Sponsor WallpaperMachine

WallpaperMachine is free and open source. The signed download costs nothing,
and the complete source is here to build.

If it has earned a place on your desktop, you can back it with a one-time
payment and become a Supporter.

  <a href="https://www.wallpapermachine.app/pricing/"><b>Become a Supporter</b></a>&ensp;·&ensp;
  <a href="https://www.wallpapermachine.app/zh/pricing/">中文定价页</a>
</p>

## Free to download. Or sponsor it.

| | Free | Supporter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | Free | One payment, no subscription |
| **The app** | The signed download, or build it from source | The same app |
| **Features** | All of them | All of them |
| **Account** | Not needed | A free account on the website |
| **Sponsor place** | No | Your name and picture on the website and in the README |
| **Priority support** | No | Your questions answered first |

The [pricing page](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/pricing/) shows the price in
your currency, tax included. Paddle.com handles the payment as reseller and
Merchant of Record.

## What a Supporter gets.

**A sponsor place.** Your name and picture on the website's
[sponsor wall](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/#sponsors) and in the
[README's Supporter list](README.md#thank-you-to-every-supporter). It stays off
until you turn it on, and you can change it from your account.

**Priority support.** Ask about a wallpaper that doesn't render right, or a
feature you would like, on the
[Supporter forum](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/support/). Your questions
are answered ahead of general requests.

## The app stays the same.

The purchase buys no software. There is no license key, no Supporter build and
nothing to unlock: the app works the same with or without it. Supporters get the
same GPL-3.0-only license as everyone else, and a refund takes none of it back. The
reasons are in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md#supporter-model-free-signed-download-paid-sponsorship-and-support).

## Before you buy.

- **One payment.** No subscription and nothing to renew.
- **14-day money-back guarantee.** The
  [refund policy](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/refunds/) explains how to ask
  for one.
- **A free account.** It links the purchase to you and lets you manage your
  sponsor place.
- **Terms.** Buying means agreeing to the
  [terms](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/terms/) and the
  [privacy notice](https://www.wallpapermachine.app/privacy/). They cover the
  purchase only; the GPL still governs the app and its source.

## Other ways to help.

- **Star the repository**, so more Mac users find it.
- **Report a wallpaper that looks wrong.** **Not working? Report on GitHub** in
  its details opens an [issue](https://github.com/TheNightlyGod/WallpaperMachine-intel/issues)
  with the wallpaper's information filled in.
- **Send a fix.** [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how to work on the
  code.
- **Tell a friend** who wants a moving desktop on their Mac.
