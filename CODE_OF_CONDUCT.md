# Code of conduct

WallpaperMachine is used and built by people who speak different languages and
know different amounts about Macs, Steam and code. Every issue, discussion, pull
request and review here should be a place where anyone can ask a question or
report a problem without being made to feel small for it.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Most reports come from someone whose desktop
  isn't doing what they hoped.
- **Write so others can follow.** Many readers use a translator. English,
  简体中文, 繁體中文 and 日本語 are all welcome.
- **Disagree with ideas, not people.** Accept the maintainers' call on what the
  project takes on, even when you would have chosen differently.
- **Respect the artists.** Credit a wallpaper by name and link. Don't post
  artwork, Workshop files or paid content you have no right to share.
- **Keep people's information private,** including what turns up in diagnostics
  reports, logs and screenshots.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity,
  background, language or experience.
- Explicit images embedded in an issue or discussion. When a wallpaper report
  needs one, describe it or link to it with a warning.
- Posting someone's private information without their consent.
- Asking for or sharing ways to pirate Wallpaper Engine, Workshop items or other
  paid software.
- Spam, advertising, and repeated off-topic or bad-faith posts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove posts, lock threads, and block people who
break these rules. Report a problem with GitHub's **Report content** on the post,
or email support@wallpapermachine.app. Reports are read by the maintainers only
and handled in confidence.

Responses follow the Contributor Covenant's ladder: a private reminder, then a
warning, then a temporary ban, and a permanent ban for severe or repeated harm.

## Scope

This applies to the repository's issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews,
the website's support forum, and anywhere someone represents the project in public.
